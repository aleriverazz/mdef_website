This research would like to continue into a new frontier: the monster as infrastructure. The Backrooms phenomenon — that internet-born mythology of liminal architectural spaces, corridors that go nowhere, the feeling of having clipped through the surface of the normal world into something always running underneath it — is not a coincidence. It arrived precisely when the digital became so embedded in daily life that physical space itself began to feel uncanny. The new monster is not a body.

It is the built environment that the system requires but refuses to acknowledge — and the Tijuana-San Diego border is the Backrooms made geopolitical: a parallel world running underneath the official cartography, where the rules of the surface do not apply and the bodies that live there know it.
